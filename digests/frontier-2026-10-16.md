  Introduces VALCON, a benchmark testing whether LLMs give consistent value judgments when switching between generator and evaluator roles, directly relevant to self-evaluation/LLM-as-judge reliability research.
- **[In the Guise of Correction: How Framing and Cultural Adaptation Erode LLM Safety on Real-World Criminal Cases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21789)** · 2026-10-16

- **[Priming Base Models for Agentic Post-Training: An Open Midtraining Recipe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22019)** · 2026-10-16
  Describes an open midtraining recipe (on Nemotron models) for priming base models toward agentic capabilities before RL post-training, relevant to understanding how agentic/RL-ready models are built.
- **[PremiseSQL: Evaluating Premise Critique and Abstention in Text-to-SQL Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22158)** · 2026-10-16
  PremiseSQL introduces a benchmark specifically testing whether Text-to-SQL systems detect flawed query premises and abstain, a transferable eval methodology for measuring calibrated refusal/abstention behavior.

  Introduces a clinically grounded mental-health support benchmark with better-behaved synthetic patients and rubrics, addressing known flaws in LLM-as-judge multi-turn eval harnesses.
- **[Evolving Together, Failing Apart: The Skill-Policy Co-evolution Trap in Self-Improving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20845)** · 2026-10-16
  Identifies a generalization gap in how self-improving agents are evaluated (scored only on the delivered skill bank), relevant to agent eval methodology.
- **[Wrong Votes, Right Gradients: Why Majority-Vote Rewards Track Verifiable RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20898)** · 2026-10-16
  Analyzes why majority-vote self-reward RL tracks verifiable-reward RL via batch-level gradient effects, directly relevant to RL and reward-signal design/reward hacking discussions.
- **[Representation Redistribution in Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21240)** · 2026-10-16

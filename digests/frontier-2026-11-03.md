# Frontier watch — 2026-11-03

## Benchmarks & methods

- **[Circuit Oracle: Automating Attribution Graph Analysis via Natural-Language Queries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00420)** · 2026-11-03
  Introduces an LLM-based multi-agent tool that automates interpretation of attribution graphs, directly reducing the manual expert bottleneck in mechanistic interpretability workflows.
- **[Random Is Almost Enough? Stable and Low-Cost Benchmark Compression via BestRand](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00883)** · 2026-11-03
  Proposes BestRand, a benchmark subsetting method with an analytical error-distribution model for reliable low-cost benchmark compression, directly useful for eval harness design.
- **[Information-Theoretic Design of Sparse Autoencoder-Based Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01378)** · 2026-11-03
  Develops an information-theoretic framework using feature-firing distributions to design more effective SAE-based activation steering interventions, advancing mechanistic interpretability methods.
- **[A Bounded Evaluation Protocol for Representational Systematicity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01755)** · 2026-11-03
  Proposes a bounded evaluation protocol to test whether neural networks are representationally (not just behaviorally) systematic, a new evaluation method relevant to probing model internals.
- **[LLMs Cannot Be Neutral: Gender Sensitivity Across Candidates, Evaluators, and Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02692)** · 2026-11-03
  Studies systematic gender bias in LLM-as-a-judge and decision-making models, directly relevant to judge reliability and fairness in LLM evaluation.
- **[IdeaTaste: Do LLMs Share the Research Taste of Human Researchers?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03424)** · 2026-11-03
  Introduces a benchmark scoring LLMs on research taste/judgment against real published work, relevant to evaluating higher-order scientific reasoning capabilities of models.
- **[Quantiles, Not Points: Streaming Quantile Matching for Ordinal-Aware LLM-as-a-Judge Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00447)** · 2026-11-03
  Proposes a new quantile-based calibration method for LLM-as-a-judge scores against human ratings, a concrete adoptable technique for improving judge reliability.
- **[From Small to Large: Assessing Medical Reasoning, Benchmark Contamination, and Model Size in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00460)** · 2026-11-03
  Investigates whether medical LLM benchmark performance reflects true reasoning versus contamination and question format artifacts, relevant to benchmark validity and eval methodology.
- **[Sparse Autoencoder Attribution Graphs Are Decided at the Margin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00606)** · 2026-11-03
  Empirical study showing SAE attribution graphs are highly sensitive to near-threshold preactivation margins, questioning reliability of a widely used mech interp tool for causal tracing.
- **[Geometry of Confabulation and Refusal in Small Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01272)** · 2026-11-03
  Introduces ConfabQA, a 784-item benchmark isolating refusal-vs-confabulation behavior and probing whether pre-generation hidden states predict and can be steered to prevent hallucinated answers.
- **[Are Fake Citations a Form of Reward Hacking? A Pre-Registered Study of Post-Training and Citation Cues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01313)** · 2026-11-03
  Pre-registered experiment across 12 open-weight checkpoints and four post-training stages testing whether citation fabrication is a measurable reward-hacking artifact of RLHF-style training.
- **[CREDO: Evidence-Conditioned Credit Assignment for Temporal Reasoning Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01643)** · 2026-11-03
  Proposes evidence-conditioned credit assignment to fix GRPO's failure to distinguish useful intermediate steps from outcome-only reward, relevant to reward hacking/RL training design.
- **[MAC-Bench: Benchmarking Multi-Agent Collaboration via Interconnected Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01728)** · 2026-11-03
  New benchmark for evaluating multi-agent LLM systems on interdependent, coordination-heavy tasks rather than isolated task difficulty.
- **[Test-Time Scaling for Open-Ended Reasoning at Small Model Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02208)** · 2026-11-03
  Proposes a judge-free semantic cluster voting method for test-time scaling on open-ended (non-math) generation tasks at small model scale, extending eval methodology beyond verifiable domains.
- **[InteractionBench: Measuring Timing, Task Accuracy, Whisper and Volume, and Grounding in Real-Time Voice Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02430)** · 2026-11-03
  New benchmark and harness jointly measuring timing, task accuracy, paralinguistic control, and grounding in real-time voice systems using shared recorded audio across systems.
- **[SelfImprove-Bench: Evaluating Generalization in Self-Improving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02797)** · 2026-11-03
  New benchmark targeting generalization of recursively self-improving agent pipelines, addressing a gap in reliable metrics for RSI capability claims.
- **[Same Outputs, Different Scores: Auditing the Unchecked Graders of Multimodal LLM Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03377)** · 2026-11-03
  Audits inconsistent, unexamined scoring/grading rules across eight multimodal LLM benchmarks, showing published scores can shift based on grader choice—directly relevant to eval methodology validity.
- **[D&A: Decoupling Description and Assessment Enables VLMs as Zero-Shot Robotic Reward Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03622)** · 2026-11-03
  Proposes a zero-shot VLM-as-reward-model method that decouples description from assessment to avoid confirmation bias/hallucinated success, relevant to reward model design and hacking in RL settings.
- **[A Survey on Agentic Security: Applications, Threats and Defenses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06445)** · 2026-11-03
  A large taxonomy (274 papers) of agentic security covering red/blue-teaming and offensive/defensive eval frameworks for agentic LLM systems.
- **[Beyond Single-shot Writing: Deep Research Agents are Unreliable at Multi-turn Report Revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13217)** · 2026-11-03
  Introduces Mr Dre, a new benchmark testing whether Deep Research Agents can reliably revise reports from user feedback across multiple turns, exposing a gap in current single-shot eval paradigms.
- **[Reinforcement World Model Learning for LLM-based Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05842)** · 2026-11-03
  Proposes a self-supervised method for learning action-conditioned world models to improve LLM agents' planning and adaptation, relevant to agentic RL training methods.
- **[Activation-Based Active Learning for In-Context Learning: Challenges and Insights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05134)** · 2026-11-03
  Tests whether transformer MLP activations can improve in-context example selection for active learning, a mechanistic-interpretability-adjacent method with potential practical adoption.
- **[SpeechEQ: Benchmarking Emotional Intelligence Quotient in Socially Aware Voice Conversational Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25990)** · 2026-11-03
  Introduces a new benchmark for evaluating emotional intelligence and paralinguistic reasoning in voice conversational models, relevant to human-AI interaction evaluation.
- **[PredActBench: Benchmarking Tool-Augmented Dialogue under Controlled Tool Noise](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02372)** · 2026-11-03
  Introduces PredActBench, a benchmark testing tool-calling agents against predictive tools with controlled noise/error rates, addressing the unrealistic assumption of perfect tool reliability in agent evals.
- **[SWE-Opt: A Benchmark for Evaluating LLMs on Performance-Oriented Repository-Level Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25530)** · 2026-11-03
  SWE-Opt is a new repository-level benchmark evaluating LLMs on real-world performance optimization trade-offs rather than isolated functions, a reusable harness for code-eval researchers.

# Frontier watch — 2026-10-23

## Benchmarks & methods

- **[FusionEval: Benchmarking Model Fusion for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30162)** · 2026-10-23
  Introduces a benchmark for evaluating model fusion techniques on long-CoT reasoning and tool use, addressing a gap where averaged scores hide capability regressions—useful for eval methodology researchers.
- **[SIM-UT: A User Simulation based Evaluation Framework for Medical Triage Chatbots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30412)** · 2026-10-23
  Proposes a user-simulation evaluation framework for multi-turn, multilingual medical triage chatbots that models confused/vulnerable users, extending eval methodology beyond single-turn English benchmarks.
- **[What the Specification Did Not Say: Specification-Oracle Gaps in Test-Graded Coding-Agent Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30635)** · 2026-10-23
  Formalizes 'specification-oracle gaps' where test-graded coding-agent benchmarks secretly require unstated upstream implementation details, a direct critique of benchmark validity relevant to eval harness design.
- **[From Clinical Judgment to Checkable Rules: Clinician-Led Guardrails for Mental-Health Chatbots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30690)** · 2026-10-23
  Describes a clinician-defined, checkable-rule alternative to LLM-as-judge for grading mental-health chatbot safety failures, offering a more interpretable eval methodology than prompt-based judging.
- **[TutorCaseBench: A Case-Grounded Benchmark for Evaluating LLM Tutors in Supporting Students with Challenging Behaviors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30828)** · 2026-10-23
  Introduces a case-grounded benchmark testing whether LLM tutors appropriately handle students with learning/behavioral difficulties, a new human-AI interaction evaluation benchmark beyond generic tutoring tests.
- **[Joint or Per-Option? Compact Encoders for Closed-Set Decisions from 17 Million to 1 Billion Parameters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30970)** · 2026-10-23
  Systematically compares joint vs per-option scoring for small encoder models on closed-set decisions across a 37-benchmark suite, offering a lightweight eval-tool alternative to LLM-as-judge routing.
- **[A Reality Check of Inference-Time Context Augmentation Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30983)** · 2026-10-23
  Empirically audits whether popular inference-time context augmentation techniques actually improve context utilization, a methodological check relevant to RAG and hallucination evaluation.
- **[What Pairwise Evaluation Misses: Reward Scores across Prompts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31090)** · 2026-10-23
  Shows pairwise reward model evaluation hides shared score components across prompts, a direct critique of standard reward model benchmarking methodology relevant to reward hacking research.
- **[Prompting Strategy Effects Depend on the Judge: A Multilingual Study of LLM-as-a-Judge Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31172)** · 2026-10-23
  Finds that prompting strategies like Chain-of-Thought for LLM-as-judge don't transfer consistently across judge models and languages, challenging common eval-harness prompt recommendations.
- **[Memory-Aware Reward Modeling: Learning Human Preferences for LLM Memory Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31272)** · 2026-10-23
  Introduces a reward model trained on ~1,000 human pairwise judgments specifically for evaluating whether LLM memory use benefits users, filling a gap in human-AI interaction and personalization evaluation.
- **[The Geometry of Metaphor: Probing and Steering Metaphor Representations in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31520)** · 2026-10-23
  Uses mechanistic interpretability (causal subspace probing) to locate and steer metaphor representations across multiple LLM architectures, directly relevant to interpretability research.
- **[Rethinking SAE Evaluation: An Atomic Interpretable Unit Framework Reveals Hidden Polysemanticity and Redundancy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30474)** · 2026-10-23
  Proposes a new 'atomic interpretable unit' evaluation framework for SAEs that exposes hidden polysemanticity and redundancy, directly relevant to mech interp eval methodology.
- **[Inherited, Not Invented: Tracing the Provenance of Wait in Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30882)** · 2026-10-23
  Traces the provenance of the 'wait' reasoning token through training data rather than RL, challenging common interpretability narratives about what RL actually teaches reasoning models.
- **[ColaBench: Evaluating Memory-Guided Knowledge Acquisition in Personal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30989)** · 2026-10-23
  Introduces ColaBench, a new benchmark for evaluating memory-guided knowledge retrieval and application in personal agents, relevant to human-AI interaction eval.
- **[SCOPE: Compiling Natural-Language Policies into Neurosymbolic Predicate Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31062)** · 2026-10-23
  Presents SCOPE, a neurosymbolic system compiling natural-language policies into predicate programs for guardrail enforcement, a new eval/safety tool addressing LLM-judge instability and cost.
- **[Interrupting the Loop: Periodic Subject Changes Raise Judged Surprise and Connection in Base Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19893)** · 2026-10-23
  Examines how prompt-loop interruptions affect LLM-judge ratings of novelty in base model outputs, bearing on the reliability of LLM-as-judge evaluation methods.
- **[Evaluating Rational Contracting in Natural Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10475)** · 2026-10-23
  Introduces a new evaluation setup for multi-turn, time-extended contracting/negotiation between language agents, extending agent benchmarks beyond one-off economic games.
- **[Scaling Laws for Generative Reward Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30765)** · 2026-10-23
  Studies scaling laws for generative reward models used as online preference providers in RL (GRPO/DPO), directly relevant to reward modeling and reward hacking dynamics.
- **[DuoCook: An Overcooked-Inspired Benchmark for Visual Planning and Coordination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31511)** · 2026-10-23
  Introduces an Overcooked-style multi-agent visual planning benchmark for vision-language models in Unreal Engine, a new eval harness for coordination and planning capabilities.
- **[EpiSEM-Bench: Evaluating Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31650)** · 2026-10-23
  New executable benchmark testing whether tool-using LLM agents act unsafely when safety-relevant facts are omitted from tool outputs, relevant to agent safety evaluation.
- **[AgentPhishing: A Benchmark of LLM Email Agents Under Adaptive Attack](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31404)** · 2026-10-23
  Benchmark testing LLM email agents' susceptibility to adaptive phishing attacks, relevant to agent security evaluation and human-AI interaction risk.
- **[VERA: A Responsible AI Dashboard for Assessing LLM Deployments in Light of the EU AI Act](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31588)** · 2026-10-23

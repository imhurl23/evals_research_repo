## Benchmarks & methods

- **[GraphNIAH: A Benchmark for Long-Context Reasoning over Real-World Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41704)** · 2026-11-02
  Introduces a needle-in-a-haystack long-context benchmark built on real-world graph data rather than synthetic text, addressing known contamination/generalization flaws in prior NIAH benchmarks.
- **[SimXAI: A Simple Drop-in Layer Increases Interaction Quality and Understanding in Conversational XAI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41856)** · 2026-11-02
  Proposes a drop-in LLM-based layer replacing brittle intent/dialogue-management pipelines in conversational explainable AI systems, directly bearing on human-AI interaction design for explanation interfaces.
- **[From Training Exposure to Membership Evidence: A Controlled Study of OLMo 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41732)** · 2026-11-02
  A controlled re-training of OLMo 2 with known document insertions to rigorously test membership-inference methods, offering a cleaner ground-truth methodology for benchmark contamination research.
- **[Beyond Public Access in LLM Pre-Training Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00020)** · 2026-11-02
  Applies the DE-COP membership inference attack to paywalled O'Reilly books to test whether OpenAI models recognize non-public training data, relevant to training-data provenance and contamination eval methods.
- **[AgentCollab: A Self-Evaluation-Driven Collaboration Paradigm for Efficient LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26034)** · 2026-11-02
  Proposes a self-evaluation-driven multi-model collaboration paradigm for LLM agents trading off execution efficiency against reasoning robustness, relevant to agent evaluation and cost-performance tradeoffs.
- **[Beyond Plausible Prediction: Safety Sufficiency in Web World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40798)** · 2026-11-02
  Human evaluation of 3,000 predictions from ten web world models reveals that plausible-seeming predictions often fail to support correct safety decisions, directly relevant to safety evaluation of agentic world models.
- **[Benchmarking Forecasting Agents on Yesterday's Tomorrows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40940)** · 2026-11-02
  New benchmark for forecasting agents using past events with known outcomes, addressing the staleness problem in live forecasting benchmarks—relevant to agentic eval methodology.
- **[P-ContextBench: From Personal Fact Recall to Personalized Agent Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40959)** · 2026-11-02
  Introduces a benchmark for evaluating whether LLMs safely and appropriately use private personal context in agent behavior, a new human-AI interaction/privacy eval harness.
- **[Confident Fools or Careful Searchers? A Behavioural Study of When LLMs Search Beyond Their Static Internal Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41124)** · 2026-11-02
  Behavioral benchmark auditing when deployed LLM agents choose to search vs. rely on parametric memory, directly relevant to agentic tool-use evaluation.
- **[From the Zoo to the BULLPEN: Sample-Efficient LLM Profiling via Multi-Channel Model Embeddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41375)** · 2026-11-02
  Proposes multi-channel model embeddings to cheaply profile many LLM properties without running full benchmark suites, a potentially adoptable eval efficiency tool.
- **[Characterizing LLM Agent Behaviors in Module Shadowing Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40716)** · 2026-11-02
  Introduces an evaluation framework to detect and characterize untrusted code execution in LLM agent trajectories, relevant to agent safety/reward-hacking style evals.
- **[From Representation to Readout: Mechanistic Analysis of Membership Signals in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41445)** · 2026-11-02
  Mechanistic study decomposing membership inference signals into internal representation vs. readout mechanisms in LLMs, directly relevant to interpretability research on memorization.
- **[GENSTRAT: Toward a Science of Strategic Reasoning in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23238)** · 2026-11-02
  Introduces procedurally-generated zero-sum betting games as a contamination-resistant benchmark for strategic reasoning in LLMs, a reusable eval method.
- **[Can Agentic Code Review Catch Vulnerabilities Before They Are Merged?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33509)** · 2026-11-02
  SecPRBench is a new benchmark testing whether agentic code review can catch real-world vulnerability-introducing commits before merge, relevant to evaluating agentic coding capability and potential reward hacking in review tasks.
- **[StrictPRMBench: A Replayable Evaluation Benchmark for Auditing Fixed-Score PRM Aggregation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41333)** · 2026-11-02
  Introduces a benchmark isolating the score-aggregation rule used in best-of-N selection with process reward models, directly relevant to reward hacking and PRM evaluation methodology.
- **[Mechanistic Interpretability for End-to-End Self-Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41185)** · 2026-11-02
  Applies sparse autoencoder-based mechanistic interpretability to an end-to-end self-driving model, a new application of SAE interpretability techniques outside LLMs that could inform methodology transfer.
- **[MIRAGE: Auditing Anti-Muslim Bias in Frontier LLMs Across Reasoning, Agentic, and Time-Coupled Conditions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16562)** · 2026-11-02
  New 1,200-prompt benchmark auditing anti-Muslim bias in frontier LLMs across single-turn, reasoning, and agentic deployment-realistic conditions, extending bias evaluation methodology.

- **[WaXup: Weight Averaging of Experts as Data Augmentation for Robust Introspection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27353)** · 2026-11-23
  Proposes weight-averaging of finetuned experts as data augmentation to improve trainable 'introspection' methods where models verbalize their own learned behavior, a mechanistic interpretability technique.
- **[Open CDLM: A Benchmark and Library for Continuous Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27536)** · 2026-11-23
  Introduces a benchmark and library standardizing likelihood- and sample-based evaluation of continuous diffusion language models, addressing known measurement flaws in comparing CDLMs.
- **[Auditing Post-Training Unlearning in Diffusion Language Models using Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27652)** · 2026-11-23
  Uses sparse autoencoders to audit whether post-training unlearning in diffusion language models actually removes targeted information, extending interpretability-based auditing beyond autoregressive LLMs.
- **[The Vulnerability Window: Decision Instability in Quantized Reward Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27155)** · 2026-11-23
  Shows that 4-bit quantization of reward models and LLM judges silently flips 1-12% of pairwise preference decisions on RewardBench despite stable aggregate accuracy, a direct warning for RLHF/reward-hacking evaluation practice.
- **[TEA-Bench-BR: Can Coding Agents Turn a Branching Clinical Specification into Correct Code?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27895)** · 2026-11-23
  Introduces a coding-agent benchmark on translating branching clinical specs to code with FHIR export, targeting silent correctness failures rather than crash-level bugs, relevant to agentic coding eval design.
- **[Improving Consistency in Retrieval-Augmented Systems with Group Similarity Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04392)** · 2026-11-23
  Proposes a group similarity reward to train RAG systems for output consistency across paraphrased queries, a concrete RL reward-shaping technique relevant to reward hacking/robustness research.
- **[CodeReviewBench: An Automated Benchmark for Evaluating the Software Factory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26942)** · 2026-11-23
  New benchmark for evaluating AI-driven code review by using actual PR authors (not third-party reviewers) as ground truth, addressing a known eval-validity gap in agentic coding pipelines.
- **[Decision Models Are General-Purpose Process Reward Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27068)** · 2026-11-23
  Introduces DM-PRM, converting general yes/no decision models into step-level process reward models for credit assignment in sparse-reward RL settings, directly relevant to RL training methodology.
- **[Benchmarks' Last Exam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27622)** · 2026-11-23
  A meta-study quantifying how often benchmark prompts, environments, and graders disagree on success criteria, directly actionable for anyone trusting leaderboard scores.
- **[Weight-space programming and mechanistic interpretability for model editing in particle physics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27079)** · 2026-11-23
  Uses sparse-autoencoder-guided mechanistic interpretability to locate and edit specific attention heads causing physics-safety violations, a concrete weight-editing application of interpretability techniques.

  Proposes a regularization method to curb reward hacking during RL post-training of diffusion models by preserving classifier-free-guidance structure, directly relevant to reward hacking mitigation techniques.
- **[Who Grades the Agent? Privilege Separation for Coding-Agent Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27860)** · 2026-11-23
  Introduces a privilege-separation design to prevent coding agents from manipulating their own grading infrastructure, directly addressing benchmark integrity and reward-hacking risks in agent evaluation.
- **[Parasite Features: Causal Abstraction Through Spurious Pathways](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26711)** · 2026-11-23
  Identifies a class of spurious 'parasite features' that pass causal abstraction equivalence tests, directly challenging a core validation method used in mechanistic interpretability.

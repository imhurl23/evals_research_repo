**PrejSense: Non-intrusive Gaze-Informed Prediction of Implicit Prejudice Against Machine for Proactive Interface Adaptation** (2026) — En-Tong Gao, Zhe Chen
[source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3830398.3830669) · joins human + rl · cites 2 map papers, 1 beyond hubs
Cites on-map: bucinca2025, ouyang2022

Proposes PrejSense, a system using non-intrusive gaze tracking to detect implicit prejudice against AI in real time and drive an LLM-agent-based adaptive interface that responds to the user's estimated bias state. It builds on human-AI collaboration and trust/cooperation framing (citing bucinca2025, ouyang2022) and extends that work toward physiological-signal-driven proactive interface design rather than retrospective self-report measures.

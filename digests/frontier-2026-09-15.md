# Frontier watch — 2026-09-15

## Benchmarks & methods

- **[Domain-Specific Jargon in Large Language Models: A Comparative Analysis between General-Purpose and Specialist Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13556)** · 2026-09-15
  Introduces two new medical jargon evaluation benchmarks probing how parametric knowledge of domain terms is encoded in general vs specialist LLMs, relevant to eval design and interpretability of knowledge encoding.
- **[Same Patient, Different Order: Action-Level Reliability of Clinical LLM Agents Under Repeated Runs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13582)** · 2026-09-15
  Proposes action-level (not just verdict-level) reliability testing for clinical LLM agents across repeated runs on MedAgentBench, exposing a blind spot in single-attempt agent benchmarking methodology.
- **[When Consistency Does Not Mean Reliability: Evaluating Local LLM Judges Against Human Ratings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13824)** · 2026-09-15
  Empirically tests whether consistent LLM-as-a-Judge scores actually correlate with human ratings using LLaMA-3-8B and Qwen2.5-7B judges, directly informing judge-based eval reliability.
- **[Policy Loopholes in Agent Evaluation: When Policy Ambiguity Masquerades as Agent Error](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14400)** · 2026-09-15
  Introduces a taxonomy of policy ambiguity in agent benchmarks like tau2-bench, showing that many 'agent errors' are actually artifacts of underspecified gold policies, a methodological critique relevant to agent eval validity.
- **[Optimizing Sparse Outcomes Through Dense Behavioral Signals via Value-Guided Preference Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14648)** · 2026-09-15
  Proposes a multi-head value model trained on dense behavioral signals to optimize long-horizon dialogue while explicitly addressing reward hacking, directly relevant to RL/reward hacking research.
- **[A primer on evaluation methods for large language models in healthcare](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14819)** · 2026-09-15
  A review synthesizing evaluation methodologies and challenges specific to deploying LLMs in healthcare, useful reference for eval researchers thinking about domain-specific validity issues.
- **[One Example Is Enough to Pass Fairness Benchmarks: Rethinking Fairness Evaluation for Aligned LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14860)** · 2026-09-15
  Shows fairness benchmarks like BBQ can be gamed with a single GRPO-trained example, a direct critique of benchmark robustness relevant to eval methodology and reward hacking.
- **[Artificial entrepreneurial cognition: Locating and causally steering an opportunity recognition dial inside large language models (LLMs)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15277)** · 2026-09-15
  Uses causal steering/probing techniques to locate an 'opportunity recognition' direction in LLM internals, a mech-interp style method applied to a novel behavioral construct.
- **[Can We Trust the Judges? Validation of Factuality Evaluation Methods via Answer Perturbation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15561)** · 2026-09-15
  Introduces a meta-evaluation framework that stress-tests factuality metrics via controlled answer corruptions, directly addressing whether current LLM eval judges are trustworthy.
- **[K-Bench: a clinically calibrated benchmark for evaluating large language models in high-risk mental health conversations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15855)** · 2026-09-15
  A clinician-calibrated benchmark testing 125 model configurations on high-risk mental health conversations, a concrete new safety-relevant eval harness for human-AI interaction research.
- **[The Limits of Reference-Free Speech Quality Metrics as Evaluators and Rewards on Modern Text-to-Speech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13150)** · 2026-09-15
  Empirically tests whether reference-free TTS quality metrics (used both as evaluators and as RL reward signals) actually track human preference, relevant to reward hacking and reward-model validity concerns.
- **[Does Reasoning Improve Psychological Depth in Large Language Models? It Depends on Who's Judging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13773)** · 2026-09-15
  Shows that LLM-judge validity for scoring psychological depth in stories breaks down depending on which judge/human rater is used, a concrete case study on LLM-as-judge reliability.
- **[Clean Scores, Buried Evidence, and Confident Wrong: A Receipt-Based Audit of Frontier Agentic QA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15319)** · 2026-09-15
  Introduces a controlled agentic QA audit showing buried evidence degrades accuracy and inflates confident wrong answers, a concrete eval methodology for measuring miscalibration and reward hacking in tool-using agents.
- **[Lost-in-the-Middle in Long-Text Generation: Synthetic Dataset, Evaluation Framework, and Mitigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06868)** · 2026-09-15
  Presents a synthetic dataset and evaluation framework for measuring and mitigating lost-in-the-middle effects in long-input-to-long-output generation, a reusable benchmark for long-context eval.
- **[Who Benchmarks the Benchmarks? Towards Comprehensive Evaluation of Commonsense Reasoning Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07825)** · 2026-09-15
  A meta-evaluation of commonsense reasoning benchmarks like HellaSwag exposing validity issues, directly relevant to researchers assessing benchmark reliability.
- **[Confident Rankings with Fewer Items: Adaptive LLM Evaluation with Continuous Scores](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13885)** · 2026-09-15
  Extends IRT-based adaptive testing to continuous LLM-as-judge scores, offering a more efficient benchmark evaluation method applicable beyond binary MCQ setups.
- **[On the Interpretability of Whisper Encodings Using Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12225)** · 2026-09-15
  Applies sparse autoencoders to Whisper's encoder to find monosemantic features, extending mechanistic interpretability methods beyond text transformers into ASR systems.
- **[Data Attribution of Emergent Misalignment with Persona Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11025)** · 2026-09-15
  Traces emergent misalignment persona features back to specific pre-training documents, directly extending mechanistic interpretability work on why fine-tuning causes broad misalignment.
- **[What's the Catch? Evaluating Temporal Consistency in Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23474)** · 2026-09-15
  Introduces TimeCatch, an anomaly-detection-based benchmark for probing whether VLMs actually capture temporal structure in video, a reusable eval method.
- **[To See is Not to Master: Teaching LLMs to Use Private Libraries for Code Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15159)** · 2026-09-15
  Shows retrieval-augmented private-library API documentation injection is insufficient for code generation and proposes a training method, relevant to LLM capability evaluation on realistic coding tasks.
- **[Transcoders Trace Visual Grounding and Hallucinations in Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22902)** · 2026-09-15
  Applies transcoders (function-centric interpretability tool) to trace how VLMs transform visual inputs into text and diagnose hallucinations, a new mech interp technique for multimodal models.
- **[Root-Cause Attribution Is a Search Problem: Continual Search for Long-Horizon Agent Failures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13463)** · 2026-09-15
  Frames root-cause attribution of long-horizon agent failures as a search problem, offering a new automated diagnostic method relevant to agent evaluation and debugging at scale.
- **[Identity Is More Than Recall: A Benchmark for Persistent Identity in Deployed AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13637)** · 2026-09-15
  Introduces PAI-Bench, a provider-neutral benchmark separating recall from behavioral enactment of identity contracts in deployed agents, a new eval methodology for persistent agent identity.
- **[Safety Signals to Verify NetOps Agents with Action-Level Granularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14422)** · 2026-09-15
  Proposes action-level safety verification signals for autonomous NetOps agents, a new methodology for evaluating long-horizon agent reliability relevant to agent eval/reward-hacking concerns.
- **[DynSTEER: Dynamic Stage-wise Trajectory Evaluation and Execution-time Review for Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14637)** · 2026-09-15
  DynSTEER introduces a stage-wise trajectory evaluation method with execution-time review to localize agent errors beyond terminal-only or single-reference matching, a new agent eval harness approach.

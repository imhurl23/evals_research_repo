# Frontier watch — 2026-10-20

## Benchmarks & methods

- **[Do LMs Know What Variables Do? Interpreting Code Representations via Variable Roles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24118)** · 2026-10-20
  Uses SAEs to test whether variable-role concepts (accumulator, traversal, etc.) are linearly recoverable and controllable in code LLMs, a concrete mech-interp probe on a non-toy domain.
- **[Ask Less, Ask Better: Allocating User Attention in Proactive LLM Agents with Interruption Budgets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24411)** · 2026-10-20
  Proposes an interruption-budget evaluation protocol that separates question frequency from question quality for proactive agents, a reusable eval method for human-AI interaction research.
- **[From Stories to Features: Scaling Concept Vectors for AI Alignment & Interpretability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24556)** · 2026-10-20
  Introduces a targeted, scalable method for extracting safety-relevant concept vectors (honesty, deception) as an alternative to full SAE feature extraction, directly relevant to alignment/interp work.
- **[Beyond Factual Accuracy: A Benchmark for Evaluating Perspective Alignment in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25087)** · 2026-10-20
  Introduces PerspectiveBench, a new benchmark isolating perspective-alignment failures that standard factuality metrics miss, a novel eval axis adoptable for LLM evaluation research.
- **[No Matter Who You Are: Addressivity and Sycophancy in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25728)** · 2026-10-20
  Mechanistically investigates how post-training's addressee-directed framing causally produces sycophantic behavior, linking interpretability to a known reward-hacking-adjacent failure mode.
- **[CALM: Consistency-Aware Logit Masking in Decoding Space for Mitigating Overthinking of Large Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25908)** · 2026-10-20
  Proposes a logit-masking decoding method to reduce overthinking in reasoning models using consistency signals rather than naive truncation, relevant to reasoning-model efficiency/behavior evaluation.
- **[LEAPS: Long-context Evaluation of Adaptive Pattern Learning and State Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25919)** · 2026-10-20
  LEAPS is a new long-context benchmark decomposing recall, state tracking, and pattern learning for agentic long-trajectory use cases, a reusable eval harness.
- **[HarnessRL: Co-Evolving Policies with a Just-in-Time Training Harness for Open-Ended RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26426)** · 2026-10-20
  Identifies concrete failure modes (reward collapse, spurious rewards) in rubric-based RL and proposes a co-evolving training harness, directly relevant to reward hacking research.
- **[Mitigating Reward Hacking in Preference Reward Models with Evolving Penalties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26496)** · 2026-10-20
  Proposes evolving penalty methods to curb reward hacking in preference reward models used in RLHF, directly on-topic for reward hacking research.
- **[HoloCosm: Beyond Single-Metric Benchmark Compression Across Multiple Metrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26714)** · 2026-10-20
  New benchmark-compression method that accounts for weak correlation across multiple evaluation metrics, useful for researchers building cheaper eval harnesses.
- **[The Judge Can Debias Itself: Awareness and Self-Debiasing for Reliable LLM-as-a-Judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24320)** · 2026-10-20
  Introduces lightweight bias-aware prompting and self-debiasing strategies to improve LLM-as-a-Judge reliability, directly useful for eval harness design.
- **[Pluralistic Alignment in Language Models: Objectives, Human References, and Preference Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24471)** · 2026-10-20
  Offers a taxonomy disentangling objectives, reference populations, and evidence standards for pluralistic alignment, relevant to human-AI interaction and preference-based evaluation design.
- **[Medical Fine-Tuning Reshapes, Not Reduces, Patient-Facing AI Risk: A Rubric-Based, LLM-Judge Evaluation Framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24807)** · 2026-10-20
  Introduces a risk-sensitive, rubric-based LLM-judge evaluation framework showing that medical fine-tuning changes rather than eliminates patient-facing harm risk, a new eval method beyond factuality scoring.
- **[SoK: PIAtlas: Decomposing the Unbounded Prompt Injection Attack Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24999)** · 2026-10-20
  Systematizes 20+ incompatible prompt injection datasets into a unified taxonomy (PIAtlas), useful as a reference structure for building or comparing red-teaming/eval benchmarks.
- **[Policy Is Not Capability: Some Safety Benchmarks Measure Which Definition of "Unsafe" You Trained On](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25060)** · 2026-10-20
  Uses a real industrial dataset labeled under two different policy definitions to show guardrail/safety benchmark scores reflect training-label policy rather than an objective 'unsafe' property, a methodological critique relevant to eval validity.
- **[VibeTraining-Bench: Data-Trainer-Harness Co-Design for Iterative System Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26342)** · 2026-10-20
  New benchmark (VibeTraining-Bench) specifically testing agents' ability to co-design data/trainer/inference harness for recursive self-improvement, directly relevant to RL training and agentic capability eval.
- **[Strength Controls Change How Interventions Compare Across Sparse Autoencoder Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26489)** · 2026-10-20
  Mechanistic interpretability paper showing that strength-matching controls (norm vs output calibration) change conclusions when comparing SAE code interventions, relevant to rigor in interp methodology.
- **[RouterWorld: Scaling Conversational AI with Latency-Aware LLM Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26511)** · 2026-10-20
  Introduces RouterWorld, a new LLM-routing benchmark addressing latency as a first-class metric and realistic partial-observability settings, a concrete eval harness improvement over existing routing benchmarks.
- **[SpatialScout: Can VLM Agents Actively Explore and Map 3D Scenes?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26659)** · 2026-10-20
  New benchmark (SpatialScout) testing whether VLM agents can actively explore and build metric 3D maps, going beyond static spatial QA.
- **[PaperAudit-Bench: Benchmarking Error Detection in Research Papers for Critical Automated Peer Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19916)** · 2026-10-20
  New benchmark (PaperAudit-Bench) with 220 papers and 2,200+ synthetic errors for testing LLM error detection in automated peer review, adoptable for scientific-reasoning eval.
- **['Your AI Text is not Mine': Redefining and Evaluating AI-generated Text Detection under Realistic Assumptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04906)** · 2026-10-20
  Proposes a reframed, more realistic definition and evaluation protocol for AI-generated text detection, challenging existing dataset/benchmark assumptions in the field.
- **[EvoBrowseComp: Benchmarking Search Agents on Evolving Knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13120)** · 2026-10-20
  New dynamic benchmark (EvoBrowseComp) targeting search-agent evaluation on evolving knowledge to counter contamination issues in static benchmarks like BrowseComp.
- **[Open-sci-ref-0.02: open reference baselines for reproducible language model and dataset comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09009)** · 2026-10-20
  Releases open reference baseline models/training runs at multiple scales to enable reproducible comparison of LM architectures and datasets, useful infrastructure for eval researchers.
- **[Bayesian Preference Learning for Test-Time Steerable Reward Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08819)** · 2026-10-20
  Proposes a Bayesian reward-model approach enabling test-time steerability of preferences, directly relevant to RL alignment and reward modeling research.
- **[Superficial Success vs. Internal Breakdown: An Empirical Study of Generalization in Adaptive Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18951)** · 2026-10-20
  Empirical study showing multi-agent LLM systems overfit to specific task topologies and exhibit internal reasoning breakdown despite surface-level success, relevant to evaluating agentic system generalization.

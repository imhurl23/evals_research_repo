  Routine version bump of the Inspect AI evaluation framework, the standard harness used for running and reporting LLM evals (including agentic/safety evals).

## Benchmarks & methods

- **[Evaluating LLMs as Agentic Workflow Executors in Real-World Customer Support Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14346)** · 2026-10-14
  Introduces a benchmark construction pipeline mining real enterprise workflows to test LLMs as constrained agentic tool-executors, a reusable eval method for agentic/tool-use evaluation.
- **[UDHRBench: Benchmarking Human Rights in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15179)** · 2026-10-14
  A new 1,124-item benchmark operationalizing LLM compliance with the UDHR, a concrete adoptable eval for values/alignment auditing.
- **[Apertus 1.5: From Open Text Models to Open Multimodal Reasoners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15542)** · 2026-10-14
  Release of Apertus 1.5, a fully open multimodal reasoning model family (8B/70B) with continued pretraining and open data, relevant as a new transparent model for interpretability/eval research.
- **[CivWorldBench: Evaluating Language World Models in Structured Multi-Agent Sandbox Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16450)** · 2026-10-14
  Introduces a benchmark for evaluating LLM world-model capabilities (hidden-state inference, intervention prediction) in a controlled multi-agent sandbox, relevant to eval methodology research.
- **[ANCHOR: Assessing Naturalistic Constraint Hierarchies and Ordering in Repository-Grounded Agentic Coding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16859)** · 2026-10-14
  A new benchmark for evaluating whether coding agents follow engineering process constraints (scope, order, validation) beyond mere functional correctness, directly useful for agentic coding eval.
- **[Agent-Reflex: Teaching Language Agents to Act Reflectively](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17953)** · 2026-10-14
  Proposes a training framework teaching LLM agents to reflect on noisy tool feedback and user corrections, relevant to RL-based agent training and failure recovery evaluation.
- **[From Agents to Edges: Paired Optimization for Multi-Agent Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18069)** · 2026-10-14
  Introduces PACO, a method jointly optimizing communication instructions between paired agents, relevant to multi-agent system design and evaluation of collaboration quality.
- **[Are We Testing Too Much? A Survey of Benchmark Compression for Efficient Evaluation of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18248)** · 2026-10-14
  Survey systematizing methods for compressing benchmarks to estimate full-benchmark scores from partial evaluation, useful for designing cheaper eval pipelines.
- **[Empathy Is Not the Scapegoat for Declines in Factuality and Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18541)** · 2026-10-14
  Empirically disentangles tone/empathy training from SFT-induced distribution shift as the real cause of factuality/reliability declines in LLMs, relevant to human-AI interaction and alignment tradeoffs.
- **[Rubrics from the Task, Verdicts from Evidence: Pointwise Rubric Rewards with Tool-Augmented Judges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18766)** · 2026-10-14
  Proposes task-derived (not response-derived) rubric reward models with tool-augmented pointwise judges to reduce bias and mechanical judging errors, directly relevant to reward model design and reward hacking.
- **[Re-executing Coding-Agent Evaluations: Unchanged Decisions and Unresolved Policy Risk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14460)** · 2026-10-14
  Re-execution study finds SWE-bench Pro's grading harness fails to reject patches despite changed grading configs, exposing concrete reliability/policy risks in widely-used coding-agent leaderboards.
- **[How to Build a Benchmark That Actually Measures Taste](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14707)** · 2026-10-14
  Introduces a new pairwise benchmark construction methodology (TasteBench) with expert-gold labels and a control protocol specifically designed to separate genuine judgment from benchmark gaming.
- **[What Do Sparse Autoencoder Feature Discovery Scores Identify? From Activations to Interventions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15850)** · 2026-10-14
  Shows that observational SAE feature-discovery scores (activation correlations) do not reliably predict intervention effects like steering/ablation, a core mechanistic interpretability methodology critique.
- **[EAU: Environment-Agent-User Co-evolution for Training User-Centric Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16523)** · 2026-10-14
  Proposes co-evolving environment, user simulator, and agent during training to address noisy/unreliable learning signals in multi-turn user-centric agent RL, relevant to agent training and human-AI interaction simulation.
- **[Evaluating the Causal Faithfulness of SHAP as a Candidate Attribution Method for SAE Features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17898)** · 2026-10-14
  Tests whether SHAP attribution on SAE features is causally faithful versus merely correlational, directly bearing on the validity of SAE-based interpretability claims.
- **[Monitoring latent reasoning in looped language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18127)** · 2026-10-14
  Introduces methods to monitor and analyze internal latent computation in looped/recurrent-depth language models, relevant to interpretability of iterative reasoning architectures.
- **[SWE-MISBEHAVIOR: Benchmarking and Mitigating Boundary Violations in Software Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18206)** · 2026-10-14
  New benchmark and mitigation framework (7.4K samples, 11 misbehavior types) for detecting boundary-violating actions in coding agents, directly relevant to reward hacking/agent safety evaluation.
- **[Readout Scaling Shapes Sparse Feature Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18462)** · 2026-10-14
  Proposes RC-DS coordinates to analyze how readout scaling affects which features SAEs recover, a methodological contribution to sparse autoencoder interpretability.
- **[Mechanistic Knobs in LLMs: Retrieving and Steering High-Order Semantic Features via Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02978)** · 2026-10-14
  Proposes an SAE-based framework for retrieving and steering high-level semantic attributes in LLM generations, contributing new steering/control techniques to mech interp.
- **[What Are We Measuring in NLG? A Meta-Analysis of Evaluation Trends 2020-2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07648)** · 2026-10-14
  Meta-analysis tracking the rapid shift toward LLM-as-judge over human evaluation in NLG papers, useful for understanding current eval methodology trends.
- **[Learning Ordinal Probabilistic Reward from Preferences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12660)** · 2026-10-14
  Proposes an ordinal probabilistic reward model that calibrates preference-based rewards, directly relevant to reward model design for RLHF and reward hacking mitigation.
- **[Soohak: A Mathematician-Curated Benchmark for Evaluating Research-level Math Capabilities of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09063)** · 2026-10-14
  A new mathematician-curated benchmark targeting research-level (beyond olympiad) math capabilities, a fresh frontier for frontier-model evaluation.
- **[Beyond Questions: Evaluating the Stated Knowledge of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26937)** · 2026-10-14
  Introduces 'open knowledge evaluation,' a new paradigm for probing LLM parametric knowledge without predefined questions, addressing availability bias in knowledge benchmarks.
- **[ForgetBench: Benchmarking Forgetting Dynamics of Long-Term Parametric Memory in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26455)** · 2026-10-14
  ForgetBench introduces a benchmark for measuring knowledge retention/forgetting dynamics under repeated model updates, a new evaluation axis beyond static knowledge editing tests.
